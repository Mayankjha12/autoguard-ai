# AutoGuard AI

## AI-Powered Predictive Supply Chain Intelligence Platform

AutoGuard AI is a supply chain resilience platform designed for the automotive industry. It helps organizations proactively identify risks, monitor suppliers, forecast disruptions, and make smarter sourcing decisions through AI-driven insights.

## Problem Statement

### Theme 1: AI for Resilient Automotive Supply Chains & Smart Manufacturing

The automotive supply chain is highly vulnerable to disruptions caused by supplier failures, logistics bottlenecks, geopolitical instability, inventory shortages, and demand volatility.

AutoGuard AI addresses these challenges by providing intelligent risk monitoring, supplier intelligence, demand forecasting, alternate sourcing recommendations, and AI-assisted decision support in a unified platform.
